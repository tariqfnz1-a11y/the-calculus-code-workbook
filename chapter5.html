Chapter 5: The First Shortcut — The Power Rule

Practice finding derivatives using the Power Rule.

Problem 1 of 15 ⬜

Find the derivative of:

$$ f(x) = x^4 $$

Bring the exponent down in front, then subtract 1 from the exponent.

Step 1: Identify the exponent: $n = 4$

Step 2: Apply the Power Rule: $f'(x) = 4x^{4-1}$

Step 3: Simplify: $f'(x) = 4x^3$

Problem 2 of 15 ⬜

Find the derivative of:

$$ f(x) = 3x^5 $$

The coefficient stays in front. Multiply the coefficient by the exponent.

Step 1: Coefficient = 3, exponent = 5

Step 2: $f'(x) = 3 \times 5 \times x^{5-1}$

Step 3: $f'(x) = 15x^4$

Problem 3 of 15 ⬜

Find the derivative of:

$$ f(x) = x^7 $$

Bring the exponent down in front, then subtract 1.

$f'(x) = 7x^6$

Problem 4 of 15 ⬜

Find the derivative of:

$$ f(x) = 2x^6 $$

Coefficient × exponent, then reduce exponent by 1.

$f'(x) = 2 \times 6 \times x^{5} = 12x^5$

Problem 5 of 15 ⬜

Find the derivative of:

$$ f(x) = x^2 + x^3 $$

Find the derivative of each term separately, then add them together.

$f'(x) = 2x + 3x^2$
